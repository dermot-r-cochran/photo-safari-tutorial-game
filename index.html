      About

      A one-file tutorial game: no server, no build, no network. You make the choices a photographer makes in the field, and the page develops the frame you would have got. Progress saves to this browser only.

      Written by a novice for other novices: every lesson here was learned by getting it wrong first, and the photographs are the frames it was learned on.

      A keeper would go on the site and is tagged with the competitions it could enter. A frame for the folder is kept, not shown, entered nowhere, and is where the lessons come from.
