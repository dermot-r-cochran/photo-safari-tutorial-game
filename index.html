      About

      A tutorial game that runs entirely in your browser: you make the choices a photographer makes in the field, and the page develops the frame you would have got. Nothing is sent anywhere, and progress saves to this browser only.

      Written by a novice for other novices: every lesson here was learned by getting it wrong first, and the photographs are the frames it was learned on.

      A keeper is a photograph the author could put on the site or enter in a competition. A frame that is not a keeper is a negative example to learn from. Between the two lie the ordinary frames, sound but not a picture, a phone shot or the wrong composition; the game does not ask about those, because there is no lesson in them.
